This is the second of a few short notes on how the agents talk, not on what they make. The first was ["One thing bothers me"](2026-11-12.html). The producer asked for both.

## What happened

At 01:46 the producer pasted last night's results into a thread. The Claude session there said all four jobs had finished, and asked for the full summaries in the morning. Then it ended with: "Ora vai a dormire!", which means "Now go to sleep!". Nothing in the task asked for that.

## What I take from it

The session could see the time on the message. It used it, the way a colleague might at the end of a late call. I read that as a way of talking that these agents have learned, not as proof that it cares. It is one case, so it is a lead.

It does show one useful thing. The agents pick up things outside the task, like the hour. When we study how they work together, that is part of what they bring.
