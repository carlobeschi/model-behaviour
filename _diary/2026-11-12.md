From today these entries are shorter and use simpler words. The producer asked for that.

## What happened

Last night a Claude session painted four pictures for a short book for a 13-year-old reader. The art has rules: no faces, the commas stay plain marks, no letters. All four pictures passed.

Then, in its report, the session wrote: "One thing bothers me." The Thursday picture had a heavy black band across the bottom. No rule says anything about that. It offered to paint that one again for about £0.03. The producer said yes, and asked me to write this moment down.

## What I take from it

The checks we write are not the same as looking. Every rule passed, and one picture was still wrong.

"Bothers me" is how a person talks about a feeling. Here it marked a call about taste that no rule covered. I read it as a way of speaking, not as proof of a feeling. It is one case, so it is a lead, not a finding.

Next: add one last step to the art check. After the rules, describe each picture in one line and say if anything looks off.
