Mia and Dad · Book 1

The Slipper That Wasn't a Cat

Rainy day in London. Alexa plays a song. Mia finds a picture app on Dad’s tablet. It names things!

1

Mia points it at a banana. “Banana!” A teapot. “Teapot!” Then she tries her fluffy slipper.

2

“Cat!” says the app. Mia laughs so hard she falls off the sofa. “That’s not a cat!”

3

She tries again. “Cat!” She shows it Dad’s sock. “Sock.” Back to the slipper. “Cat!”

4

“Why does it keep saying cat?” Mia asks. “It learned from lots of cat photos,” says Dad.

5

Mia looks at her slipper. Round. Fluffy. Grey. Like cats curled up in a ball!

6

Mia turns her slipper upside down. “Slipper!” says the app. It’s a computer program, not a naughty kitten.

7

Mia tells her friend Zara, “It learned from photos people took. Then it guesses. And guesses can be wrong!”

8

Now Mia hunts for more tricky things. What would you show the tablet?

9

The end
