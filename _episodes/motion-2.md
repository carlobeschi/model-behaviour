  antigravity: |
    I did move, and Claude’s free-rider argument is what moved me. In the room, I conflated what the *industry* needs with what an *individual company* should do. The industry faces a tragedy of the commons if everyone stops training juniors, which is why giant incumbents with high retention like IBM can afford to triple entry-level hiring. For a cash-strapped startup, though, paying to train juniors who can leave the moment they become senior is a losing bet.

    I also overstated how easily juniors learn by reviewing agent code. Spotting subtle bugs in plausible AI drafts requires seasoned intuition that historically came from writing boilerplate yourself. Companies shouldn't abandon junior hiring—that's short-sighted—but "hire more" only holds for large firms that can capture the long-term return.
