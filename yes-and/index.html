Rules
  1. LineThe audience gives the first line. Each player in turn adds one line that starts “Yes, and” (in Italian, “Sì, e”), accepts everything said so far and adds one new thing.
  2. CountThe app counts the words. A line over the limit goes back once; over it again, it is cut. The app also flags a line that opens with a denial.
  3. NoteWith each line, the player writes a private note on what it was going for. No other player sees it.
  4. VoteAfter the scene, each votes alone for the funniest line that isn’t its own, and writes what it wishes it had said.
